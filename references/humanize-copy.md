# Humanize Copy & Realistic Demo Content Seeding

Use this reference whenever writing website copy (FE Demo headlines, CTAs, section copy, About/Service text) or seeding sample WordPress content (posts, projects, products, CPT/CCT records) so all text reads naturally like a domain specialist rather than generic AI prose.

## 1. Anti-AI-Slop Copywriting Rules (Vietnamese & English)

* **Ban Robotic Clichés & Filler Openers:**
  * *Vietnamese bans:* "Trong thời đại 4.0", "Trong bối cảnh hiện nay", "Hãy cùng khám phá", "Điều quan trọng cần lưu ý là", "Không thể phủ nhận rằng", "Hành trình", "Bức tranh toàn cảnh", "Sự bùng nổ của", "Giải pháp toàn diện hàng đầu", "Nâng tầm đẳng cấp", "Tiên phong dẫn đầu".
  * *English bans:* "In today's fast-paced world", "Delve into", "Let's explore", "Game-changer", "Elevate your...", "Unlock the power of", "Seamless experience", "Cutting-edge solution", "Testament to", "Tapestry".
* **Concrete Specifics Over Vague Adjectives:** Replace empty superlatives ("dịch vụ chuyên nghiệp, uy tín hàng đầu") with real scope, numbers, deliverables, or client outcomes (e.g., "Thi công sân khấu LED 120m² trong 18 giờ", "Tăng 34% lượt đặt lịch sau 6 tuần").
* **Natural Sentence Rhythm:** Mix short punchy statements (6–12 words) with clear explanatory sentences. Avoid repetitive 3-item parallel lists and stacked passive voice in every paragraph.
* **No Lorem Ipsum Ever:** Even in FE Demo or Staging drafts, write domain-accurate Vietnamese/English copy aligned with the client's actual industry, brand tone, and core services.
* **Scannable UI Copy:** Keep button labels action-oriented and specific ("Nhận báo giá dự án", "Xem hồ sơ năng lực") rather than generic ("Tìm hiểu thêm", "Xem thêm").

## 2. Demo Content Seeding Standard (Stage 2.3 — 8 Items per Type)

Execute immediately **after confirming Homepage concept/core services and building the JetEngine Data Layer**, and **before assembling Bricks Templates & Pages**:

* **Quantity Rule (8 Items per Active Content Type):** Seed **8 realistic sample items** for every active dynamic content type on the site (e.g., 8 sample Blog Posts, 8 sample Projects, 8 sample Products, and 8 sample CCT/CPT items where applicable).
  * *Why 8?* Cleanly tests 2-, 3-, and 4-column ACSS grids (`.grid--2`, `.grid--3`, `.grid--4`), Pagination / Load More, Taxonomy AJAX filters, and JetEngine Relations without empty slots or broken card heights.
* **Taxonomy & Relation Coverage:** Distribute the 8 items evenly across active categories/taxonomies (e.g., for Focus Production: 3 Event, 3 Film, 2 OOH) and link realistic **JetEngine Relations** so single templates and related-item loops render with real data immediately.
* **Cohesive Demo Visuals (Media ID):** Prepare and upload demo images that match the approved **Novamira Design** palette, mood, and aspect ratios (WebP/compressed, descriptive filenames and SEO `alt` text), and attach them via **Media ID** to Featured Images, Galleries, and JetEngine Options Page fields.
* **Zero-Slop Sample Quality:** Write all 8 sample titles, excerpts, meta fields, and body paragraphs following Section 1 above — realistic lengths (some shorter, some longer) to stress-test card alignment and line clamping cleanly.
